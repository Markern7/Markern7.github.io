Purpose

This utility supports the account owner's private project file transfers and explicitly authorised processing. It is not a public storage service and provides no third-party access to the owner's files.

Authorised use

Use the utility only with accounts and files you are permitted to access, store and process. Choose the intended destination account before transferring data. Do not place passwords, access tokens or other credentials in ordinary data uploads or public repositories.

Operation and availability

The utility is provided as available, with no guarantee of uninterrupted transfers. Google and GitHub service limits, authentication expiry and connectivity may affect operation. Maintain local backups and verify transfer receipts before relying on a cloud copy.

Costs and third-party services

The utility itself does not collect subscription fees or payments. Google storage subscriptions and any GitHub service charges are governed by those providers' terms and the owner's chosen plans. This website does not purchase services automatically.

Privacy and stopping use

The Privacy Policy explains the app's data handling. The owner may stop using the utility, revoke Google authorisation and remove stored credentials at any time. Existing files remain until separately removed by the owner.

Contact

For configuration questions, use the website issue tracker without including private data or credentials.
